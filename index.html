Tournament Rules

  1. Each team must have a minimum of 6 and a maximum of 8 players.
  2. Registration is confirmed only after payment of the Rs. 500 entry fee.
  3. The remaining balance of Rs. 3000 must be paid before the team's first match.
  4. The entry fee is non-refundable in case a team withdraws.
  5. Teams must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match.
  6. A team not present within 15 minutes of the scheduled time will be disqualified from that match.
  7. The decision of the referee/umpire on the court will be final and binding.
  8. Any form of misconduct with officials or opposing teams may lead to disqualification.
  9. The organizing committee reserves the right to change the schedule, format, or rules if required.
  10. Open only to teams and players belonging to the Pahalgam region; outside teams/players are not permitted.
